# As-Is and To-Be System Models

## 1. As-Is Model (Traditional Restaurant Ordering)

In the traditional model, ordering relies heavily on physical interactions and staff availability.

**Process Flow**:
1. Customer sits at a table.
2. Customer waits for a waiter to bring a physical menu.
3. Customer browses the menu.
4. Customer waits to flag down the waiter.
5. Waiter takes the order on a notepad or handheld POS.
6. Waiter walks to the POS station or kitchen to input the order.
7. Kitchen prepares food.
8. Waiter delivers food.

**Drawbacks**: High labor cost, slow service during peak hours, prone to human error, physical menus get dirty/damaged.

## 2. To-Be Model (QR Onsite Ordering Platform)

The proposed system digitizes the menu and ordering process, transferring control to the customer's device.

**Process Flow**:
1. Customer sits at a table.
2. Customer scans the QR code on the table using their smartphone.
3. Customer immediately browses the digital menu.
4. Customer adds items to cart and reviews upsell suggestions.
5. Customer confirms order, which instantly generates a WhatsApp message.
6. Order is received simultaneously on the shop's WhatsApp device.
7. Kitchen prepares food.
8. Staff delivers food directly to the specified table number.

**Benefits**: Immediate access to menu, reduced waiter workload, integrated digital upsells, elimination of complex POS hardware requirements.\n
