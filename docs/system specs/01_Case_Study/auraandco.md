# Case Study: AuraAndCo

## 1. Profile
**AuraAndCo** is a premium, modern restaurant focusing on aesthetic presentation and high-quality dining experiences.

## 2. Challenges
- Traditional printed menus do not effectively showcase the visual appeal of their premium dishes.
- The restaurant wants to increase the average order value by suggesting wine pairings and side dishes.

## 3. Implementation of QR Platform
AuraAndCo adopted the platform focusing heavily on the visual menu and upsell features.
- **Upsell Relationships**: Every main course is linked to suggested sides and premium beverages (e.g., Steak -> Red Wine).
- **Outcome**: The digital menu allows AuraAndCo to present high-resolution images of their dishes. The automated upsell prompts have successfully increased the average ticket size, and the digital experience aligns with their modern brand image.\n
