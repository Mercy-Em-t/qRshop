# Case Study: MamaRosy

## 1. Profile
**MamaRosy** is a busy, fast-paced local eatery that experiences high customer turnover during lunch hours. 

## 2. Challenges
- Waitstaff are overwhelmed during peak hours, leading to delayed order taking.
- Customers often ask for the same popular menu items, but the menu is paper-based and frequently gets damaged or lost.

## 3. Implementation of QR Platform
MamaRosy deployed the QR Onsite Ordering Platform with QR codes pasted directly on the dining tables.
- **Menu Structure**: Simplified, single-category menu prioritizing daily specials.
- **Outcome**: Order processing time decreased significantly. Customers scan the QR, order their lunch, and the kitchen receives the WhatsApp message instantly. The need for waitstaff to run back and forth just to hand out menus has been eliminated.\n
