# Case Study: Kahakai6

## 1. Profile
**Kahakai6** is an expansive beachside café where seating is spread out across a large area, including outdoor cabanas and loungers.

## 2. Challenges
- Waiters have to walk long distances between the kitchen and the beachside tables.
- It is difficult for customers to flag down a waiter when they want to order additional drinks or snacks.

## 3. Implementation of QR Platform
Kahakai6 utilized the platform to generate location-specific QR codes for every cabana and table.
- **Location Verification**: The optional location verification feature ensures orders are only placed by customers physically at the beach club.
- **Outcome**: Customers can order additional drinks at their convenience without waiting for a server. The WhatsApp orders specify the exact cabana number, making delivery highly efficient for the staff.\n
