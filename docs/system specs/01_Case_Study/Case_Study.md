# Case Study: QR Onsite Ordering Platform

## 1. Project Background
Restaurants, cafés, and sports venues often face challenges with slow table service, miscommunicated orders, and the high cost of traditional Point of Sale (POS) systems. Customers experience friction waiting for waiters to bring menus, take orders, and process payments.

## 2. The Solution
The **QR Onsite Ordering Platform** provides a streamlined, self-service ordering experience. Customers scan a QR code placed on their table to access a digital menu, select items, and place an order directly. The system bypasses complex backend integrations by utilizing WhatsApp for order transmission, allowing businesses to receive orders instantly on a platform they already use.

## 3. Key Objectives
- **Frictionless Access**: Customers access the menu instantly via QR code without downloading an app.
- **Operational Efficiency**: Shop owners manage menus digitally and receive orders formatted clearly via WhatsApp.
- **Revenue Growth**: The system supports automated upsell suggestions (e.g., suggesting fries with a burger) to increase average order value.

## 4. Target Audience
- **Customers**: Patrons who want to order food quickly and accurately.
- **Shop Owners**: Small to medium-sized F&B businesses looking for a low-cost, easy-to-implement ordering system.
- **System Admins**: Platform managers overseeing multiple shops and subscriptions.\n
